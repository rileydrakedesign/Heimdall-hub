# References (patterns, not pixels)

Goal: collect layout + hierarchy references that fit a modern SaaS landing with a dark theme.

## Reference set
1) Vercel marketing pages
- Strong hierarchy, high-contrast surfaces, tight spacing system.

2) Linear
- Clear hero, crisp feature cards, restrained motion.

3) Raycast
- Strong product screenshots, clean demo sections.

4) Notion
- Benefit-led sections and scannable layout.

## Patterns to borrow
- Hero with one strong promise + one proof line + primary CTA
- Demo section early (show, don’t tell)
- 3-step “how it works” with matching visuals
- Feature cards with icons and tight copy
- FAQ focused on trust and adoption blockers
