This is a v0 plan derived from today’s research sweep inputs.

---

## 1) Agent for X

### ICP (starting wedge)
- Solo creators / indie builders posting on X daily (1–3 accounts) who want: consistency, voice-preserving drafting help, scheduling, and *safe* light automations.

### Positioning (one-liner)
- “Write in your voice, publish consistently, and automate safely—with visible limits and anti-spam guardrails.”

### Differentiation bets (what to ship/market)
1) **Automation Safety Policy** (public page) + “why these caps exist” (trust + conversion).
   - Competitive proof that users look for this: https://tweethunter.io/competitors/tweethunter
2) **Limits dashboard** (in product): show X accounts, scheduling horizon, automation quotas, and remaining budget.
   - Competitive pattern (workflow-coupled limits): https://socialrails.com/blog/hypefury-pricing
3) **Voice preservation controls** (explicit “voice sliders” / constrained rewrites) as the trust primitive.

### Pricing hypothesis
- Anchor with a low-cost solo plan (like Typefully’s $8/mo starter) and a “creator sweet spot” plan, while being explicit about workflow-limits.
  - Reference: https://socialrails.com/blog/typefully-pricing

### Channels
- Affiliate program is a real growth loop in this category; ship an official affiliate kit (copy, assets, policy, limits page).
  - Reference affiliate mechanics: https://hypefury.crisp.help/en/article/what-is-the-hypefury-affiliate-program-sus7v3/

### 14-day execution slice
- Day 1–2: draft + publish Automation Safety Policy + caps.
- Day 3–5: implement limits dashboard (even if static first).
- Day 6–10: ship “voice lock” rewrite mode (minimal UI; measurable).
- Day 11–14: affiliate kit + 20 outbound to micro-creators who teach “X growth” (offer rev-share early).

## 2) Class Chat AI

### ICP (starting wedge)
- Individual instructors + TAs in courses with high question volume (STEM/quant) who are drowning in repeated questions and DM fragmentation.

### Positioning (one-liner)
- “Course Q&A that becomes a searchable knowledge base—with privacy-forward defaults and instructor controls.”

### Differentiation bets
1) **Dedupe at composition time** (“similar questions” suggestions) + strong categories.
   - Reference: https://support.wharton.upenn.edu/help/ed-discussion-for-students
2) **Anonymous-but-accountable** modes (anonymous to peers; staff-visible) + endorsements/resolution state.
   - Reference: https://support.wharton.upenn.edu/help/ed-discussion-for-students
3) **Trust story**: explicitly contrast with Piazza-style privacy narratives; lead with no-selling/no-training-by-default + deletion.
   - Reference: https://stanforddaily.com/2020/10/04/concerned-with-piazzas-data-privacy-management-some-professors-look-to-alternative-discussion-forums/

### Pricing hypothesis
- If going paid: align with procurement-shaped unit expectations (per student/per course) OR offer a free core with paid governance/integrations.
  - Pricing pressure reference: “$6 per student per course” reported for Ed: https://stattlc.com/2022/07/29/using-ed-discussion-as-a-course-communication-tool/
  - Free anchor reference: https://campuswire.com/

### Channels
- Instructor-led adoption first, but plan for Canvas/LTI early because “institutional proof” is how paid wins.
  - Canvas integration reference: https://support.wharton.upenn.edu/help/ed-discussion-for-students

### 14-day execution slice
- Day 1–3: prototype “ask → suggested similar threads” + category requirement.
- Day 4–7: add anonymity modes + resolution/endorsement.
- Day 8–10: publish privacy posture page (plain language + retention/deletion controls).
- Day 11–14: recruit 3 instructors/TAs for pilot; run “DM fragmentation” audit (baseline repeated Qs).
