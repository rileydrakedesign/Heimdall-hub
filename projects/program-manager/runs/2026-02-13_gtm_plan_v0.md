## 1) Agent for X — v0 GTM

### ICP (narrow first)
- Solo creators + indie founders who post on X for distribution.
- Pain: inconsistent posting + fear of “AI voice” + fear of platform penalties + dislike of opaque limits.

### Positioning (one sentence)
- “Write in your voice, automate safely with visible guardrails, and understand exactly what your limits are.”

### Differentiators to prove (fast)
1) **Voice lock** (reduce ‘generic AI tone’)
2) **Automation Safety Facts** (caps + why + audit log)
3) **Transparent meters** (credits/caps visible; no surprise throttling)

### Pricing hypothesis (grounded in today’s evidence)
- Base: per-account (creator) pricing.
- Add-on: **X add-on per connected account** (mirrors suite behavior; clear unit).
  - Evidence (X add-on model): Metricool prices Twitter/X add-on at $5/month per connected account.
  - Source: https://metricool.com/pricing/
- AI writing: use explicit monthly credits/caps with dashboard visibility.
  - Evidence (AI credits): Later uses AI credits as the unit for AI caption/ideas.
  - Source: https://later.com/pricing/
- Note: platform economics: X API is pay-per-usage; packaging must map “automation actions” → cost.
  - Source: https://developer.x.com/en/docs/twitter-api/rate-limits

### Channels
- X itself: short proof clips (before/after voice diffs + safety caps screen).
- Creator tool newsletters / communities: pricing transparency is a hook (“no surprise throttles”).

## 2) Class Chat AI — v0 GTM

### ICP (narrow first)
- Teachers / instructional coaches piloting AI, plus the district admin who must approve.

### Positioning
- “A classroom chat assistant that is safe-by-default and adoption-ready (SSO/LMS/DPA), not ‘just another chatbot.’”

### Pricing/packaging norms to match
- Per-educator seat for individual/school adoption; **custom district tier** when SSO/SIS/LMS + DPA/legal are required.
  - Evidence: MagicSchool Plus pricing + district custom tier.
  - Source: https://www.magicschool.ai/pricing
- Compliance/certs are part of conversion copy (not a late-stage security page).
  - Evidence: SchoolAI pricing highlights FERPA/COPPA + SOC2 + 1EdTech.
  - Source: https://schoolai.com/pricing

### Trust posture (must ship with GTM)
- A teacher-facing “Do/Don’t upload” guide and a procurement-facing privacy packet.
  - Evidence baseline for teacher caution: Common Sense warns not to input sensitive data into gen-AI.
  - Source: https://www.commonsense.org/education/reviews/chatgpt
  - Legal baseline: FTC COPPA compliance steps.
  - Source: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/childrens-online-privacy-protection-rule-six-step-compliance-plan-your-business

## Recommended next GTM action (do this next)

**Agent for X: ship a public “Limits + Automation Safety Facts” page and link it everywhere (pricing, onboarding, X thread).**

Why this is the next compounding move:
- Today’s evidence shows the market expects explicit meters (Later credits) and platform add-ons (Metricool’s X add-on).
- Making limits visible flips a fear (“I’ll get throttled/banned”) into a trust feature.

Minimum content checklist:
- what automations exist (and don’t)
- per-day/per-hour caps + why they exist
- safe mode (human approval)
- audit log + deletion/retention
- API usage model & what happens when you hit limits
