## 1) Agent for X (priority #1)

### Positioning (tight)
Write and ship X posts **in your voice**, with transparent safety controls (caps, previews, and clear “what we don’t do”) so you can stay consistent without account-risk anxiety.

### Differentiation to prove (in-market)
1) **Voice preservation** (anti-generic output).
2) **Safety + security posture as a feature**
   - Say “Is this safe?” out loud and answer it.
   - Evidence lane: competitors explicitly handle safety objection in FAQ copy.
   - Source: https://taplio.com/pricing
3) **Full-loop workflow** (ideation → drafts → schedule → performance feedback → reuse)
   - Evidence lane: Taplio positions as a few-minutes/day cockpit + free companion extension.
   - Source: https://taplio.com
4) **Usage visibility (credits/quotas) if/when agentic workflows expand**
   - Evidence lane: “agent” products increasingly show monthly credits + overages + monitoring.
   - Sources: https://clickup.com/pricing , https://www.atlassian.com/licensing/rovo

### Core assets (next 72h)
1) **“Trust & Controls” micro-page / section**
   - “What we do” vs “What we don’t do” (no hacks; no credential storage; no spam)
   - Default caps + user-editable caps
   - Preview-before-post (and an audit log of what was posted)
2) 20–30s demo clip
   - voice prompt → 3 drafts → schedule → show caps/preview → publish
3) Free companion wedge
   - lightweight Chrome extension (or bookmarklet) that shows: draft helper + queue preview + safety caps

### Channels (first tests)
- X build-in-public: launch the “Trust & Controls” page with a short demo clip
- Partner/affiliate outreach (micro-creators): include “safe automation” FAQ + demo + swipe copy
- SEO demand capture: “X post ideas”, “X post templates”, “X scheduler safe” (route into the safety story)

### One experiment (start now)
Landing A/B:
- A: “Write in your voice”
- B: “Safe posting: caps + previews so you never look like spam”
Measure:
- visit → waitlist
- clicks on “Trust & Controls” section

## 2) Class Chat AI (priority #2)

### Positioning (tight)
District-ready class chat where answers are grounded (RAG) and adoption is easy (LMS/SSO), with explicit student-data commitments and admin visibility.

### Differentiation to prove
1) **District rollout path** (LMS/SSO + fewer tabs)
   - Evidence lane: MagicSchool markets LMS embedding + SSO as the default adoption route.
   - Source: https://www.magicschool.ai/integrations
2) **Governance + observability** (admin controls, usage dashboards, audit traces)
   - Evidence lane: SchoolAI pricing page foregrounds compliance + certifications + observability features.
   - Source: https://schoolai.com/pricing
3) **Clear student-data commitments** (plain-English “we do not…”)
   - Evidence lane: MagicSchool privacy policy lists what they explicitly do not do (sell/share/train/ads).
   - Source: https://www.magicschool.ai/privacy-policy
4) **Deep trust documentation** (retention, training defaults, embeddings story)
   - Benchmark lane: Notion AI publishes specific retention + “no training by default” + embeddings explanation.
   - Source: https://www.notion.com/help/notion-ai-security-practices

### Assets to build
1) Admin-ready “Trust Pack” page:
   - retention + deletion
   - training defaults
   - subprocessors
   - compliance posture (SOC2 roadmap, FERPA/COPPA posture)
2) LMS/SSO integration narrative:
   - “launch inside Classroom/Canvas” equivalent (even if manual v0)
3) Teacher onboarding:
   - “start in 30 seconds” + templates + example Spaces

### Channels
- District admin outbound (email + LinkedIn): lead with governance + compliance + rollout simplicity
- Teacher communities: lead with “reduce tabs + reduce burnout” (extension / in-workflow story)
