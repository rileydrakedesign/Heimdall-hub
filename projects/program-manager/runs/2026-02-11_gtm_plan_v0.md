## 1) Agent for X — GTM v0

### Positioning (1 sentence)
An agentic workflow for X creators that helps you go from idea → draft → schedule → engage, with **safe automation** (caps, approvals, and provenance) so you don’t risk your account.

### ICP (v0)
- Solo/indie founders, operators, and creators posting on X 3–7x/week.
- Secondary: small teams (2–10) managing multiple X accounts/brands.

### Core promise
- Faster throughput without losing voice.
- Safer automation than “growth hacks”: **draft-first**, explicit caps/cooldowns, and a visible audit log.

### Pricing hypothesis (mapped to competitor norms)
- Tier axes to test:
  1) # of accounts/brands (“social sets”)
  2) AI output quota (posts/replies per month)
  3) Scheduling horizon (e.g., 1–12 months)
  4) Team permissions + audit log (team/agency tiers)
- Competitive anchors:
  - Typefully Creator/Team/Agency: $12.50/mo → $49/mo → $99/mo (yearly billing shown)
    - Source: https://typefully.com/pricing
  - Postwise: $37/mo → $59/mo → $97/mo with quotas + “months scheduling” and “custom AI training” at top tier
    - Source: https://postwise.ai/pricing
  - Tweet Hunter sells a bundled “growth OS” including automation (Auto-DM/Auto-retweet/etc.)
    - Source: https://tweethunter.io/pricing

### MVP scope (what must exist to compete)
- Draft editor + scheduling queue
- Basic analytics (post performance)
- Automation features (minimal but safe): e.g., “auto-plug” / “auto-reply suggestions” behind caps
- Trust primitives:
  - Audit log (who/what created/edited/scheduled)
  - Provenance labels: human vs API/agent
  - Approval workflow for anything that posts

### Channels (first 30–60 days)
1) **Comparison pages** (high-intent SEO): “Agent for X vs Tweet Hunter / Typefully / Postwise”
   - Typefully already invests in “Compare” pages linked from pricing.
   - Source: https://typefully.com/pricing
2) **Affiliate program (creator-to-creator)**
   - Create a minimal affiliate landing + terms + dashboard.
   - Benchmark: Typefully affiliate program.
   - Source: https://support.typefully.com/en/articles/8718317-typefully-s-affiliate-program
3) **Lead magnet + onboarding**
   - Bundle “playbook” assets (like Tweet Hunter University) with trial to accelerate time-to-value.
   - Source: https://tweethunter.io/pricing

### Proof strategy
- Public “Safe Automation” page that addresses ToS/ban anxiety explicitly.
  - Benchmark for why this matters: Tweet Hunter terms call out ToS compliance and abuse.
  - Source: https://tweethunter.io/terms-of-use
- Screenshots / short clips showing audit log + approvals.

## 2) Class Chat AI — GTM v0

### Positioning (1 sentence)
A study assistant that lets students upload course materials and ask questions with **citations** so answers can be verified.

### ICP (v0)
- University students in reading-heavy courses.
- Secondary: TAs/instructors looking for Q&A support (longer-term).

### Pricing hypothesis
- Keep the current low monthly anchor and emphasize trust:
  - Free: tight quotas (uploads/questions)
  - Paid: “unlimited” + citations + priority support
  - Source: https://classchatai.com/pricing/

### Key objections to handle
- “Does it actually answer from *my* docs?” → citations UX, excerpt previews, and clear limitations.
- “Is this credible?” → marketing polish and product trust pack.
  - Source: https://classchatai.com/

### Competitive benchmarks (monetization + trust)
- Piazza: instructor license per term + department licensing; explicitly reassures “no ads”.
  - Source: https://support.piazza.com/support/solutions/articles/48001161300-paid-model-for-piazza-q-a

### Workflow expectations if leaning into “class chat”
- Consider moderation primitives (queueing, upvotes, polls).
  - Source: https://campuswire.com/livesessions
