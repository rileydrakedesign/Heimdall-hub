## Agent for X — GTM takeaways (today)
- **Competitive packaging:** users buy “the loop” (library + scheduling + evergreen queue + automation like Auto-DM/Auto-retweet + analytics), not a writer.
  - Source: https://tweethunter.io/pricing
- **Pricing axes that resonate:** (1) number of accounts/brands, (2) AI output quota, (3) scheduling horizon (months), (4) team permissions.
  - Sources: https://postwise.ai/pricing , https://typefully.com/pricing
- **Trust/objection to preempt:** ToS compliance and ban anxiety is explicit in competitor terms; guardrails are a feature.
  - Source: https://tweethunter.io/terms-of-use
- **Workflow expectations:** integrations + agent surfaces are now mainstream (API/Zapier/MCP/webhooks + Raycast). Teams expect provenance (“MCP/API created this”).
  - Sources: https://typefully.com/changelog , https://support.typefully.com/en/articles/13128440-typefully-mcp-server

## Class Chat AI — GTM takeaways (today)
- **Pricing hook:** “citations” is the explicit upgrade lever; free tier quotas are tight (uploads + questions).
  - Source: https://classchatai.com/pricing/
- **Category trust bar:** marketing polish matters because the promise is “verified answers”; low-signal template content weakens credibility.
  - Source: https://classchatai.com/
- **Enterprise ladder benchmark:** term-based instructor licensing + department licenses are normal; “no ads” is a trust anchor in learning.
  - Source: https://support.piazza.com/support/solutions/articles/48001161300-paid-model-for-piazza-q-a
- **Workflow adjacency:** if positioned as “class chat”, buyers may expect moderation primitives (queueing, upvotes, polls, breakouts).
  - Source: https://campuswire.com/livesessions
