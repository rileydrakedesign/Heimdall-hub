- Solo creators + indie founders who post on X for distribution and can’t risk “robotic automation” perception.
- Secondary: ghostwriters / content ops people running multiple accounts.

### Positioning (one sentence)
- “Write in your voice and automate safely — with explicit caps and an approval workflow that prevents ‘robotic’ behavior.”

### Differentiators to prove (fast)
1) **Voice Lock + Diff Preview** (make “AI needs editing” cheap)
   - Evidence that AI output needs editing is common.
   - Source: https://www.nichepursuits.com/tweet-hunter-review/
2) **Safe Mode (Approval + Caps + Audit Log)** (turn the trust objection into a feature)
   - Evidence: competitor ecosystem acknowledges automation can feel “robotic.”
   - Source: https://kawaak.com/blog/en/postwise-alternatives-us/
3) **Tier ladder that maps to real knobs** (AI writer, automation bundle, custom model)
   - Evidence: Tweet Hunter ladder + upsells.
   - Source: https://www.nichepursuits.com/tweet-hunter-review/

### Channels
- X (obviously): daily short clips of “Voice Lock + Diff” + “Safe Mode caps screen” + “queue preview.”
- Affiliate/referral motion: creator-tool buyers actively seek coupons/deals.
  - Evidence: coupon/affiliate pages act as pricing surfaces.
  - Source: https://aiwriters.tools/ai-writers/tweet-hunter/

## Recommended next GTM action (do this next)

**Agent for X: ship a public “Automation & Safety Contract” page + in-product Safe Mode (approval + caps) and link it from onboarding + pricing.**

Why this is the compounding move:
- Today’s evidence says the category’s biggest objection is “robotic automation.”
- A shareable artifact travels on X and also arms affiliates/referrers with a concrete trust hook.

Minimum spec (one page + one screen):
- explicit list of automations (and what you will never do)
- per-hour/per-day caps + why they exist
- Safe Mode approval workflow
- audit log (what happened, when, and why)
- data handling: what’s stored, retention, deletion
