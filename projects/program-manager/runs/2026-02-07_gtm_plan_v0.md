## 1) Agent for X (priority #1)

### Positioning (tight)
Write and ship X posts **in your voice**, with “safe automation” guardrails that keep you consistent without risking your account.

### Differentiation to prove (in-market)
1) **Voice preservation** (anti-generic output).
2) **Automation you can trust** (guardrails + transparency): rate limits and explicit constraints.
   - Evidence lane: competitors productize anti-abuse posting caps and explain them.
   - Source: https://publer.com/plans
3) **Distribution loop** (draft → schedule → follow-up) as a system, not a generator.
   - (Prior evidence lane still holds: TweetHunter loop packaging.)

### Core assets (next 72h)
1) “Safe Automation Policy” micro-page (or landing section)
   - What we do / what we don’t do
   - Default caps (posts/day) + user control
   - Clear “preview before post” step
2) 20–30s demo clip: write → schedule → safety caps UI → publish preview
3) Affiliate-ready creator pack (to support partners)
   - Swipe copy, demo script, before/after, FAQs
   - Evidence lane: affiliate programs ship assets + longer trials for partner audiences.
   - Source: https://tweethunter.io/affiliates

### Channels (first tests)
- X build-in-public: “Safe Automation” explainer + demo clip
- Affiliate/partner outreach: 10 micro-creators with a ready-to-post pack
- SEO demand capture (templates/schedulers): publish 1 “tweet templates” or “scheduler” piece and route into the product
  - Evidence lane: template/scheduler content is used as a funnel into the tool.
  - Sources: https://typefully.com/blog/twitter-post-templates , https://typefully.com/blog/tweet-schedulers

### One experiment (start now)
Landing A/B test:
- A: “Write in your voice”
- B: “Safe automation: consistent posting without risk”
Measure:
- visit → waitlist
- scroll depth to Safety section

## 2) Class Chat AI (priority #2)

### Positioning (tight)
Ask questions about your class PDFs and get answers you can **verify instantly** (trace back to the exact sentence/table cell), with clear privacy controls.

### Differentiation to prove
1) **Tracing granularity** (beyond generic citations): page → paragraph → sentence-level.
   - Evidence lane: competitors push “sentence/word-level tracing” as the standard.
   - Source: https://chatdoc.com/blog/source-tracing-in-ai-document-q-a-making-every-answer-verifiable-with-chatdoc/
2) **Teach users to ask better questions** (because phrasing + token limits drive quality).
   - Evidence lane: competitors publish guidance + recommend “small batches / be specific.”
   - Source: https://chatdoc.com/blog/chatdoc-guide-how-to-make-good-queries/
3) **Concrete privacy posture** (not vibes): deletion control + encryption + named audit posture.
   - Evidence lane: SOC2 + encrypt + delete are explicit conversion signals.
   - Source: https://www.humata.ai/privacy-policy

### Assets to build
1) 3 demo clips (10–15s each): answer → highlight claim → jump to source → “copy citation”
2) “How to ask” onboarding: templates + auto-suggested follow-ups (threaded)
3) Privacy/controls page: what’s stored, retention, delete button behavior

### Channels
- Short video demos (verify loop)
- SEO: “with citations” + “verify from PDF” + “flashcards with sources”
