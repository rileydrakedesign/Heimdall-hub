## 1) Agent for X (priority #1)

### Positioning (tight)
Turn raw input (notes/transcripts/links) into X posts that **keep your voice** — plus a workflow that keeps you consistent (draft → schedule → distribute → measure).

### Differentiation to prove (in-market)
- Voice preservation (anti-generic) *and* workflow polish.
- A credible “system” story: creators buy time back (minutes instead of hours).
  - Evidence lane: competitor copy sells time relief + automation.
  - Source: https://tweethunter.io/
- Safety stance: explicit, user-controlled automation (rate limits, transparency).
  - Evidence lane: “Is it safe?” shows up as a primary objection in adjacent tools.
  - Source: https://taplio.com/pricing

### Core assets (next 72h)
1) **Voice-preserving before/after pack** (5 examples)
2) **Distribution Loop demo clip** (20–30s): draft → queue/schedule → “follow-up” step (reply/DM) → simple metric
3) Landing variant that sells the loop:
   - Headline draft: “Write in your voice. Ship consistently. Don’t babysit X.”

### Channels (first tests)
- X build-in-public: before/after + loop clip
- Creator DMs: offer “3 posts from your last long-form piece” in exchange for 10 min feedback
- Extension wedge (optional): free micro-tool (hook generator / rewrite) to seed top-of-funnel
  - Analog: Taplio promotes a free extension as companion surface.
  - Source: https://taplio.com/

### Experiment (start now)
- A/B landing CTA framing:
  - A: “Keep your voice”
  - B: “Minutes not hours (distribution loop)”
- Measure: visit → waitlist; plus DM reply rate

## 2) Class Chat AI (priority #2)

### Positioning (tight)
Answers from your class PDFs **with citations you can verify fast** (study lane, not legal/financial).

### Differentiation to prove
- Trust UX: citations + side-by-side verify
  - Evidence lane: ChatPDF copy makes this the core promise.
  - Source: https://www.chatpdf.com/
- Control UX: delete docs + clear retention story
  - Evidence lane: AskYourPDF exposes delete endpoints + citation toggles.
  - Source: https://docs.askyourpdf.com/
- Scope clarity: explicitly study/homework/research; avoid overpromising for proprietary/legal.

### Assets to build
1) 3 demo clips (10–15s each): cite → jump to source → highlight quote
2) “Privacy + Control” mini-page: retention, delete button, what’s stored
3) Prompting helper UI: question templates (“Find definitions”, “Explain like I’m 15”, “Give me flashcards with citations”)

### Channels
- SEO long-tail (study + citations)
- Short video demos
