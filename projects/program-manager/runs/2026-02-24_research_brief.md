## Agent for X — what matters now

### What competitors train buyers to expect
- “All-in-one” loop: **inspiration library → drafting → queue/scheduling → safe automations → analytics** (not AI writing alone).
  - Source: https://tweethunter.io/competitors/tweethunter
- Limits are **workflow-shaped**: X-account caps, scheduling horizon, auto-DM ceilings, repurposing quotas.
  - Source: https://socialrails.com/blog/hypefury-pricing
  - Source: https://socialrails.com/blog/typefully-pricing

### Main objections to neutralize
- “Will this get me banned / shadowbanned?” + “is it spammy?”
  - Source: https://tweethunter.io/competitors/tweethunter
- “Hidden caps” frustration (limits buried until you hit them).
  - Source: https://socialrails.com/blog/hypefury-pricing

### Implication for positioning
- Lead with **Automation Safety Policy + explicit caps + rationale** (trust artifact), and ship a **limits dashboard** in-product.

## Class Chat AI — what matters now

- Category anchor: “**free forever**” for core course comms.
  - Source: https://campuswire.com/

  - Source: https://support.wharton.upenn.edu/help/ed-discussion-for-students

  - Source: https://stattlc.com/2022/07/29/using-ed-discussion-as-a-course-communication-tool/
- Workflow requirements that matter: categories/subcategories, anonymous options, endorsements/resolution, similar-thread suggestions.
  - Source: https://support.wharton.upenn.edu/help/ed-discussion-for-students

### Trust narrative (distribution amplifier)
- Piazza privacy controversy is a real switching moment; alternatives explicitly market “not selling your info.”
