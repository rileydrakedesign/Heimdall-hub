## 1) Agent for X — v0 GTM

### ICP (narrow first)
- Creators/indie founders posting on X daily.
- Already tried at least one “growth suite” tool (Tweet Hunter/Hypefury/Typefully/etc).
- Primary blockers:
  - fear of account risk,
  - uncertainty about what is/isn’t automated,
  - surprise limits.

### Positioning (one sentence)
- “Safe automation with explicit caps — written in plain English — plus one-click pause/revoke.”

### Proof points to ship (fast)
1) **Canonical Automation Safety Policy page (v1)**
   - “No hacks; normal-usage behavior” claim in plain language + how we enforce it.
   - Explicit caps/budgets and what happens when you hit them.
   - “What we never do” list.
   - Inspiration for the copy style (pricing FAQ-level):
     - https://taplio.com/pricing

2) **Full-loop competitor framing**
   - Show which parts of the loop we cover (ideas/drafts/schedule/engage) and what we deliberately don’t.
   - Competitor reference point for buyer expectations:
     - https://tweethunter.io/pricing

### Channels (next 2 weeks)
- “Safety page” as a shareable artifact:
  - Turn it into the link you can drop in replies, DMs, and affiliate content.
  - Run a micro-campaign: 10 creators, 10 DMs, 10 public reply drops (manual), each pointing to the safety page.

## 2) Class Chat AI — v0 GTM

### ICP (narrow first)
- Instructors/TAs in Q&A-heavy courses (or parents buying a study assistant), *and* an upgrade path for districts.

### Positioning
- “A study/chat assistant with enterprise-grade privacy posture — without enterprise friction.”

### Proof points to ship (fast)
- **Packaging by buyer (copy this structure):** teacher-free wedge → learner/family subscription → district request pricing with SSO/rostering/analytics.
  - Source:
    - https://www.khanmigo.ai/pricing
- **Legible trust statement** (“we don’t sell student data”) + FERPA-friendly posture.
  - Source:
    - https://medium.com/campuswire/campuswire-faqs-c6993d93cb29
- **Benchmark privacy posture language** (no-training-by-default, encryption, retention controls).
  - Source:
    - https://openai.com/business-data/

## Recommended next GTM action (do this next)

**Agent for X: publish a canonical Automation Safety Policy page (plain English) + explicit caps + self-serve pause/revoke UX, then use that one link as the core outbound/affiliate collateral for 2 weeks.**

Why this compounds:
- It converts the #1 objection (account risk) into a product surface.
- It becomes reusable collateral across channels (DMs, replies, affiliates, docs).
- It forces the product to define “scope boundaries” clearly, which reduces support burden.
