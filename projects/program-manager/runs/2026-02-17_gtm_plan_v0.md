## 1) Agent for X — v0 GTM

### ICP (narrow first)
- Solo creators + indie founders running X as their primary distribution channel.
- High sensitivity to: account safety, token scopes, and “robotic automation” backlash.

### Positioning (one sentence)
- “Write in your voice and automate safely — with transparent permissions, explicit limits, and instant revocation.”

### Differentiators to prove (fast)
1) **Permissions & Revocation UX** (trust surface)
   - Show requested scopes, why, and a 1-click revoke.
   - Evidence: X OAuth 2.0 flow + refresh tokens (`offline.access`) is an explicit choice that needs explanation.
   - Source: https://docs.x.com/fundamentals/authentication/oauth-2-0/authorization-code
2) **Public data-handling stance** (what we store, retention, deletion)
   - Evidence: Typefully’s privacy policy explicitly discusses API keys + deletion windows + encryption.
   - Source: https://typefully.com/privacy
3) **Affiliate-ready artifact**
   - Create an authoritative “Pricing + Limits + Safety Contract” page so affiliates don’t route buyers through stale third-party pricing pages.
   - Evidence: official affiliate programs are common in this niche.
   - Source: https://support.typefully.com/en/articles/8718317-typefully-s-affiliate-program

### Channels
- X (product clips): “scope screen” + “revoke screen” + “queue preview” + “caps rationale.”
- Affiliate/referral: launch after the artifact exists; copy/paste link kit.

## 2) Class Chat AI — v0 GTM

### ICP (narrow first)
- Course instructors/TAs and small departments who need safe discussion/Q&A tooling.
- Secondary: district buyers who require governance (SSO, auditability).

### Positioning
- “Classroom chat with adult oversight and auditability — designed for FERPA/COPPA reality.”

### Differentiators to prove (fast)
1) **Observability is the trust primitive**
   - Transcript visibility, monitoring dashboards, alerts.
   - Evidence: SchoolAI markets real-time monitoring and complete transparency.
   - Sources:
     - https://schoolai.com/trust/student-safety
     - https://help.schoolai.com/en/articles/10270403-monitor-student-activity-with-mission-control
2) **Proof-oriented privacy posture**
   - “No training / deletion guarantees” + procurement-ready artifacts.
   - Evidence: MagicSchool’s public explainer emphasizes provider deletion + “zero data retention” attestations.
   - Source: https://www.magicschool.ai/blog-posts/ai-data-privacy

## Recommended next GTM action (do this next)

**Agent for X: publish a “Permissions + Safety Contract” page + ship a “Connected Accounts” settings screen (scopes, last-used, revoke).**

Why this compounds:
- It directly neutralizes the highest-friction trust question (“what access do you have to my X account, for how long, and how do I undo it?”).
- It becomes a canonical link for future affiliates and X threads, reducing misinformation from third-party pricing/deal pages.

Minimum spec (deliver in 1 sprint):
- A public page:
  - requested scopes + plain-English explanations
  - whether refresh tokens/offline access are used
  - automation limits + why they exist
  - retention + deletion + breach posture
- In-app settings:
  - list connected accounts
  - last token refresh / last publish timestamp
  - revoke button (with confirmation + what breaks)
