## 1) Agent for X — v0 GTM

### ICP (narrow first)
- Solo creators + indie founders who post on X daily.
- Already tried at least one tool (Typefully/Tweet Hunter/etc.) and either:
  - hate generic AI voice, or
  - fear account risk / automation safety, or
  - want engagement leverage (replying) without “becoming a spammer.”

### Positioning (one sentence)
- “In-context replies in your voice, with visible budgets and published safety caps.”

### Proof points to ship (fast)
1) **Posting Budget UX** (because upstream is pay-per-usage)
   - Show a “posting budget” meter + explicit caps (per day/week/month) + cooldown behavior.
   - Source (upstream economics): https://docs.x.com/overview

2) **Workflow completeness without suite bloat**
   - Ship just enough “loop coverage” to compete with Tweet Hunter:
     - drafts/queue + evergreen recycle
     - lightweight “people lists” for reply targeting
   - Keep it composable (Zapier/webhooks/MCP) so we avoid turning into a full social suite.
   - Source (competitor baseline): https://tweethunter.io/pricing

3) **Affiliate-ready trust artifact**
   - Before opening an affiliate loop, publish a canonical “Limits + Safety + Permissions + Revocation” page.
   - Evidence that affiliate motion is standard (and tiered): https://hypefury.crisp.help/en/article/what-is-the-hypefury-affiliate-program-sus7v3/

### Channels (next 2 weeks)
- X content that’s *product proof*:
  - “Here are my caps and why (account protection)”
  - “Here is exactly what we automate vs don’t automate”
  - “Pause + revoke access in one click”
- Only after the trust page exists:
  - recruit 5–10 creators for a small affiliate pilot

## 2) Class Chat AI — v0 GTM

### ICP (narrow first)
- Instructors/TAs of large STEM courses (lots of duplicate questions; high TA load).
- Currently on Piazza/Ed/Campuswire and open to switching *if trust + workflow wins are obvious*.

### Positioning
- “A Piazza replacement that proves answers with citations and treats student data like a liability, not an asset.”

### Proof points to ship (fast)
- **Replace-Piazza narrative + consolidated workflow** (what the category expects)
  - Competitor positioning: “Replaces: LMS forums, Piazza” + bundled comms stack.
  - Sources:
    - https://campuswire.com/
    - https://medium.com/campuswire/campuswire-faqs-c6993d93cb29
- **Trust posture that’s explicit, not vibes**
  - Competitor copy leads with student-data privacy / incentive alignment.
  - Sources:
    - https://medium.com/campuswire/campuswire-vs-piazza-a-breakdown-e9a9d99896d8
    - https://stanforddaily.com/2020/10/04/concerned-with-piazzas-data-privacy-management-some-professors-look-to-alternative-discussion-forums/

## Recommended next GTM action (do this next)

**Agent for X: ship a public “Posting Budget + Limits + Safety” page *and* a tiny in-product “posting budget meter” UI.**

Why this compounds:
- Converts the biggest objection (account risk + opaque limits) into a feature.
- Aligns packaging with upstream pay-per-usage economics.
- Unblocks affiliate/partner growth without letting third-party pages define our safety story.

Minimum spec (1 sprint):
- Public page:
  - caps + cooldown rules
  - what we automate vs what requires human approval
  - how to pause + revoke
  - data retention/deletion + security contact
- Product UI:
  - budget meter + warning thresholds
  - 1-click pause
  - 1-click revoke
