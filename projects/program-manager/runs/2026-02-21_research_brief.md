# Research Brief — 2026-02-21

Priority order:
1) **Agent for X**
2) **Class Chat AI**

## What changed / what we learned today (high signal)

### 1) X is being packaged as a paid carve-out (buyer expectations are trained)
- Metricool excludes Twitter/X from its free plan and sells X as an **add-on (+$5 per connected account)**.
- Implication for Agent for X: we can be explicit about X-specific costs/limits and still be “normal” — as long as we explain it clearly.
- Source:
  - https://metricool.com/pricing/

### 2) “Is it safe?” is now a pricing-page FAQ (automation fear is a primary objection)
- Taplio (LinkedIn automation, but highly analogous) explicitly answers “Is Taplio safe?” with: “behaves exactly like a real user… doesn’t rely on hacks,” framing guardrails as account protection.
- Implication: Agent for X needs a linkable artifact: **Automation Safety Policy** + explicit caps + revoke/pause, not vague reassurance.
- Source:
  - https://taplio.com/pricing

### 3) Typefully’s privacy policy is conversion copy masquerading as legal text
- Typefully explicitly claims it won’t open drafts/posts unless asked; gives deletion timelines (purge within 30 days) and describes encryption posture.
- Implication: “Security/Privacy/Retention/Revocation” language should live in top-of-funnel pages and onboarding.
- Source:
  - https://support.typefully.com/en/articles/8718444-privacy-policy

### 4) Campus AI competitors are selling “scale + privacy + security requirements,” not just tutoring
- ChatGPT Edu is positioned as “bring AI to campus at scale,” with a university CIO quote emphasizing student privacy + research/IP + security requirements.
- Implication: Class Chat AI needs procurement-grade language + artifacts earlier (even in SMB/department motion).
- Source:
  - https://chatgpt.com/business/education

### 5) FERPA grounding should be explicit (canonical .gov resources exist and are linkable)
- The Student Privacy (ED.gov) FERPA hub links directly to statutes/regulations and guidance.
- Implication: our privacy docs should map product behavior → FERPA concepts (records, consent, redisclosure, retention).
- Source:
  - https://studentprivacy.ed.gov/ferpa

## Implications

### Agent for X
- Product:
  - Publish **Automation Safety Policy** (caps, cooldowns, what we automate vs don’t, revoke/pause, retention/deletion).
  - Add **posting budget meter** (visible caps) to match “X add-on / upstream cost” expectation.
- GTM:
  - Lead with safety + transparency, then voice/quality.

### Class Chat AI
- Product:
  - Procurement pack lite: FERPA language, retention/deletion defaults, and a clear “we do/don’t train on your data” statement.
- GTM:
  - Position vs “just use Discord” by emphasizing governance + course structure + admin visibility.
