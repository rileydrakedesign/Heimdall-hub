## 1) Agent for X — v0 GTM

### ICP (narrow first)
- Solo creators + indie founders posting on X for distribution.
- Secondary: small teams running a brand account who want safe delegation.

### Positioning (one sentence)
- “Write in your voice, schedule ahead, and automate safely with explicit limits that protect your account.”

### Differentiators to prove (fast)
1) **Voice lock + diff preview** (don’t smother voice)
2) **Scheduling horizon tiers** (make “plan 90 days ahead” a paid unlock)
3) **Guardrails that prevent spammy behavior** (caps, approvals, audit log)

### Pricing hypothesis (grounded in today’s evidence)
- Simple tier ladder like Postwise:
  - tier by AI output volume + **scheduling horizon** (3mo / 12mo / unlimited)
  - Source: https://postwise.ai/pricing
- Trust language must be explicit and placed where conversion happens:
  - competitors answer “Is it safe?” directly in pricing (Taplio)
  - Source: https://taplio.com/pricing
- Product must address the known objection: automation can become spammy.
  - Source: https://softwareoasis.com/tweet-hunter-review-monetize-twitter/

### Channels
- X itself: short demos of (a) voice-lock rewrite, (b) safety caps screen, (c) approval workflow.
- Creator tool newsletters: hook is “automation without the spammy feel.”

## 2) Class Chat AI — v0 GTM

### ICP (narrow first)
- University instructors + TAs who already use PDFs/notes and want Q&A with citations.
- Secondary: departmental buyers who require compliance posture.

### Positioning
- “A course materials chat assistant with citations and admin-ready governance — safe-by-default for FERPA reality.”

### Packaging norms to match
- Multi-dimensional limits shown in the plan table (avoid surprises): pages/doc, upload size, docs/day, questions/day, conversations/day.
  - Evidence: AskYourPDF pricing table.
  - Source: https://askyourpdf.com/pricing

### Trust posture (must ship with GTM)
- A faculty-facing FERPA + gen-AI checklist:
  - data minimization defaults
  - consent guidance
  - vendor agreement language + transparency
  - Evidence baseline: faculty FERPA guidance.
  - Source: https://nmu.edu/ctl/understanding-ferpa-context-generative-ai-guide-faculty

## Recommended next GTM action (do this next)

**Agent for X: publish a one-page “Automation Safety Facts + Limits” page and wire it into onboarding and pricing.**

Why this is the compounding move:
- Today’s evidence shows safety is a *conversion question* (Taplio answers it on pricing), and over-automation is a known objection (Tweet Hunter review).
- A clear “behavioral contract” reduces fear-based churn and becomes a shareable artifact in X threads.

Minimum content checklist:
- what automations exist (and don’t)
- explicit per-day/per-hour caps + why
- Safe Mode (human approval) + audit log
- data retention/deletion for connected accounts
- what happens at limits (pause, queue, request approval)
