## 1) Agent for X — v0 GTM

### ICP (narrow first)
- Solo creators + indie founders who post on X daily.
- Already tried at least one tool (Tweet Hunter / Typefully / suites) and are blocked by:
  - fear of account risk (automation/throttling), and/or
  - confusion about limits/budgets, and/or
  - dislike of generic AI voice.

### Positioning (one sentence)
- “Safe automation in your voice — with explicit caps, budgets, and one-click revoke.”

### Proof points to ship (fast)
1) **Automation Safety Policy (public, linkable)**
   - What we automate vs what requires approval
   - Caps/cooldowns + “why” (account protection)
   - One-click pause + revoke instructions
   - Data retention/deletion + security contact
   - Evidence this is a market-standard FAQ surface:
     - https://taplio.com/pricing

2) **X carve-out transparency (pricing + product UX)**
   - If X usage is cost-driven, say so plainly; buyers already see X as an add-on in other tools.
   - Evidence buyers are trained for X add-ons:
     - https://metricool.com/pricing/

3) **Trust copy that is specific (not vibes)**
   - Mirror Typefully-style explicitness: don’t open drafts/posts unless asked; concrete deletion window.
   - Source:
     - https://support.typefully.com/en/articles/8718444-privacy-policy

### Channels (next 2 weeks)
- Ship the policy + budget UI, then run content that is *product proof*:
  - “Here are the caps and why.”
  - “What we never do.”
  - “Revoke access in one click.”

## 2) Class Chat AI — v0 GTM

### ICP (narrow first)
- Instructors/TAs of large courses (lots of duplicate questions; high TA load).
- Already using Piazza/Campuswire/Discord/Slack mixes.

### Positioning
- “Course chat that’s FERPA-aware by design, with governance and auditability.”

### Proof points to ship (fast)
- **Procurement-grade trust primitives (lite)**
  - FERPA mapping + retention/deletion defaults + “training/no-training” statement in plain English.
  - Canonical FERPA hub:
    - https://studentprivacy.ed.gov/ferpa
- **Competitive anchor: campus AI at scale is sold on privacy/security requirements**
  - ChatGPT Edu’s positioning sets expectations for institutional readiness.
  - Source:
    - https://chatgpt.com/business/education
- **Beat “just use Discord” by focusing on governance + structure**
  - Discord privacy posture sets a baseline; we win via course workflows + admin controls.
  - Source:
    - https://discord.com/privacy
