## 1) Agent for X — v0 GTM

### ICP (narrow first)
- Solo creators + indie founders who live on X.
- Already paying for social tooling (suite or creator tool) but anxious about **account risk** and annoyed by dashboard bloat.

### Positioning (one sentence)
- “In-context replies in your voice, with published safety caps and 1-click revocation.”

### Proof points to ship (fast)
1) **Public Limits + Safety artifact** (canonical, linkable)
   - Publish a “fair use” style page that spells out caps + cooldown rules + what happens when you hit thresholds.
   - Evidence that this is normal: competitors publish explicit thresholds and enforcement steps.
   - Source: https://help.metricool.com/en/article/fair-use-policy-for-social-media-scheduling-oh90gv/

2) **Token/permission transparency** (trust conversion)
   - Plain-English explanation of OAuth scopes requested, token lifetime, whether `offline.access` is used, and how to revoke.
   - Source: https://docs.x.com/fundamentals/authentication/oauth-2-0/authorization-code

3) **X-specific packaging clarity**
   - Consider making “X features” or “X analytics” a clearly-labeled module if X costs/limits differ (buyers are already trained for this via add-ons).
   - Source: https://metricool.com/pricing/

### Channels (next 2 weeks)
- X threads + short clips demonstrating:
  - “here are the exact caps and why (account protection)”
  - “pause automation + revoke access”
  - “voice controls” (before/after)
- Partner/affiliate later (only after the canonical artifact exists).

## 2) Class Chat AI — v0 GTM

### ICP (narrow first)
- Instructors/TAs who currently use Discord/Slack informally, but face institutional concerns.

### Positioning
- “Official-classroom-safe discussion + chat with governance, auditability, and LMS integration.”

### Proof points to ship (fast)
- **Institutional trust wedge**: some universities explicitly do not recommend Discord for official classroom activity due to FERPA/privacy posture.
  - Source: https://oit.colorado.edu/services/web-content-applications/discord
- **Procurement checklist readiness**: LTI integrations trigger DPA + privacy policy + accessibility + retention/destruction concerns.
  - Source: https://www.bcm.edu/education/academic-faculty-affairs/faculty-resources/faculty-ed-tech/learning-management-systems/blackboard/blackboard-ultra-course-view/lti-tool-integration-requests

## Recommended next GTM action (do this next)

**Agent for X: ship a public “Limits + Safety + Permissions” page (with explicit thresholds + revocation instructions) and link it from every product surface.**

Why this compounds:
- It directly answers the #1 purchase objection (ban/throttle + long-lived access fear) with a canonical, shareable artifact.
- It lets you turn necessary guardrails into a marketing advantage ("we protect your account") instead of a hidden limitation.

Minimum spec (1 sprint):
- A public page:
  - cadence caps and fair-use thresholds (and what triggers cooldown)
  - OAuth scopes requested + whether `offline.access` is used
  - where tokens are stored + how to revoke + what revocation changes
  - retention/deletion + security contact
- A tiny UI surface:
  - “Pause automation” + “Revoke access” + “My posting budget” indicator
