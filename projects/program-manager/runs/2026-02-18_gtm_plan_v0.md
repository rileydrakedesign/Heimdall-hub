## 1) Agent for X — v0 GTM

### ICP (narrow first)
- Solo creators + indie founders who live on X and fear account risk.
- People already paying for growth tooling (Tweet Hunter / Typefully / Hypefury class) but annoyed by dashboard bloat.

### Positioning (one sentence)
- “In-context replies in your voice, with explicit safety caps and instant revocation.”

### Proof points to ship (fast)
1) **Safety Contract page** (canonical)
   - “what we do / what we never do”
   - scope list + whether offline access is used
   - cadence caps + why (account protection)
   - retention/deletion + breach posture
2) **Affiliate-proof collateral**
   - a short, official affiliate kit (approved claims + screenshots + limits) to prevent misinformation.
   - Evidence: Tweet Hunter’s affiliate program ships a full enablement package + asset pack.
   - Source: https://tweethunter.io/affiliates
3) **Safety copy that matches category language**
   - adopt “no hacks/shortcuts; normal usage patterns” framing (plus explicit technical guarantees where we can).
   - Evidence: Taplio’s pricing FAQ uses exactly this template.
   - Source: https://taplio.com/pricing

### Channels
- X short clips: “safety caps rationale” + “revoke access” + “voice controls.”
- Affiliate/referral: launch once the canonical Safety Contract page exists.

## Recommended next GTM action (do this next)

**Agent for X: ship a public “Pricing + Limits + Safety Contract” page *plus* an affiliate-ready kit (approved claims + screenshots + a 1-page limits table).**

Why this compounds:
- Affiliates are already normalized in this category and will otherwise route buyers through stale third-party pages.
- The #1 purchase objection (“is this safe / will I get throttled/banned”) is best answered with a stable artifact that can be linked in DMs, threads, and partner content.

Minimum spec (1 sprint):
- A public page:
  - scopes requested + plain-English explanation
  - whether refresh tokens/offline access are used (and how to revoke)
  - automation caps + what triggers cooldowns
  - data retention + deletion controls
- A downloadable affiliate kit:
  - approved claims + prohibited claims
  - screenshots + logos
  - a canonical pricing/limits table
