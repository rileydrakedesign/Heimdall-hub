## 1) Agent for X — v0 GTM

### ICP (narrow first)
- Solo creators + indie founders posting on X daily.
- Have tried at least one tool already (Typefully/Tweet Hunter/Hypefury/etc).
- Primary blockers:
  - fear of account risk (automation/throttling),
  - distrust of “generic AI voice,”
  - surprise/hidden limits.

### Positioning (one sentence)
- “Safe automation in your voice — with explicit budgets, self-serve pause/revoke, and zero-surprise limits.”

### Proof points to ship (fast)
1) **One canonical public page: Safety + Limits + Pricing**
   - Explicit automation scope (what we do vs never do)
   - Explicit caps/budgets + “why” (account protection)
   - Self-serve pause/revoke instructions + delete/retention policy
   - This page becomes affiliate-safe collateral.

2) **Package like the market already does**
   - Accounts + monthly usage quota + scheduling horizon + voice controls.
   - Evidence: tiers are literally defined this way in creator tooling.
     - https://postwise.ai/pricing

3) **Risk reversal as a feature**
   - Consider mirroring “self-serve refund/cancel/pause” expectations with self-serve revoke/delete.
   - Evidence: BlackMagic uses self-serve refunds (14 days) + self-serve subscription management.
     - https://blackmagic.so/pricing

### Channels (next 2 weeks)
- Affiliate-native distribution:
  - Recruit 5–10 micro-affiliates (creators with newsletters) and give them the canonical page + swipe copy.
  - This aligns with how Typefully instructs affiliates to drive signups (threads/profiles/newsletters/videos).
    - https://support.typefully.com/en/articles/8718327-earn-with-typefully-s-affiliate-program

## 2) Class Chat AI — v0 GTM

### ICP (narrow first)
- Instructors/TAs running Q&A-heavy courses.
- Already tried Slack/Discord/Piazza/Campuswire-style tools.

### Positioning
- “Course Q&A that reduces DM fragmentation, supports safe anonymity, and stays aligned with student privacy expectations.”

### Proof points to ship (fast)
- **DM-fragmentation → public learning loop**
  - Lean into the instructor pain: DMs prevent shared answers and scale.
  - Source (instructor report):
    - https://stattlc.com/2022/07/29/using-ed-discussion-as-a-course-communication-tool/
- **Incentive alignment headline**
  - “We’re a paid product; we don’t sell student data” (say it plainly).
  - Privacy controversies create switching moments.
  - Source:
    - https://stanforddaily.com/2020/10/04/concerned-with-piazzas-data-privacy-management-some-professors-look-to-alternative-discussion-forums/
