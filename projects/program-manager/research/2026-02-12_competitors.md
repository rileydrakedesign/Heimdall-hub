## Iteration 1 — “Creator scheduling + AI writer” packaging baseline

### Agent for X — direct competitors / adjacents

**Taplio (LinkedIn-focused; heavy automation + lead DB)**
- Packaging pattern: 3 tiers (Starter/Standard/Pro) with **AI credits** and **comment credits**, then “Unlimited” at top tier.
- Pro tier leans into growth automation: auto connection requests, auto-DM, mass DMs.
- Trust objection is answered explicitly (“Is Taplio safe?”) with “behaves like a real LinkedIn user” framing.
- Source: https://taplio.com/pricing

**Tweet Hunter (X-focused; growth stack + AI writer + CRM)**
- Packaging pattern: viral tweet library + scheduling + automations (auto-DM/auto-plug/auto-retweet) + analytics; upsell to “BEST-IN-CLASS AI” with custom model training.
- Source: https://tweethunter.io/pricing

**Typefully (X/LinkedIn/Threads; best-in-class writing UX positioning)**
- Pricing surfaced via third-party review: free tier + annual Creator/Team/Agency tiers.
- Notable wedge: “don’t get sucked into your feed” + drafting/collab.
- Source: https://efficient.app/apps/typefully

**Circle (community platform; now bundling ‘AI Agents & AI workflows’ at enterprise tier)**
- Packaging: Professional ($89/mo), Business ($199/mo), Circle Plus (custom) adds **AI Agents & AI workflows**.
- Source: https://circle.so/pricing

### Class Chat AI — competitor proxy (adjacent) signal
- Circle’s bundling of “AI Agents & AI workflows” suggests buyers accept “AI agents” as an *enterprise add-on* once community/workflows are already bought.
- Source: https://circle.so/pricing

## Iteration 2 — “Class Q&A tools” and edu monetization norms (paid, per-student/term)

### Class Chat AI — direct competitors / adjacents

**Ed Discussion (Piazza alternative; institution-paid, term-based norm)**
- Painful-but-normal pricing for departments: **“$6 per student per course”** cited by instructor blog; no free tier.
- Packaging expectations: LTI integrations, anonymous posting, moderation/endorsement, searchable Q&A.
- Source: https://stattlc.com/2022/07/29/using-ed-discussion-as-a-course-communication-tool/

**Piazza (trust/privacy controversy as a competitive opening)**
- Piazza Network opt-in described as enabling employer access to profile/course/role data; drives instructor switching.
- Source: https://stanforddaily.com/2020/10/04/concerned-with-piazzas-data-privacy-management-some-professors-look-to-alternative-discussion-forums/

### Agent for X — positioning implication from edu tools
- Instructors explicitly compare Slack/Discord vs Q&A tools based on *workflow primitives*: anonymous posting, endorsement, LaTeX, categories.
- If “Agent for X” is an agentic workflow tool, it needs similarly crisp primitives (“approve”, “queue”, “guardrails”, “voice lock”) not just “AI writing.”
- Source: https://stattlc.com/2022/07/29/using-ed-discussion-as-a-course-communication-tool/

## Iteration 4 — “Privacy policy as conversion copy” (explicit data handling)

### Agent for X — trust / data posture competitive benchmark

**Typefully privacy policy (explicit access model + encryption + retention)**
- Claims: only access your account for support/bugfix; collects X/LinkedIn API keys; TLS in transit; backups encrypted; deletion purge within ~30 days.
- This is *conversion copy*, not just legalese: addresses the “what do you store / who can see drafts” objection directly.
- Source: https://typefully.com/privacy (redirects to Typefully Help Center privacy policy)

### Class Chat AI — same pattern
- Circle privacy policy: clear distinction between platform vs creator/community data controller/processor roles.
- Source: https://circle.so/privacy
