## Iteration 1 — Automation tools must answer “Is this safe?” + API-key/privacy objections

### Pain points / objections
**Agent for X**

## Iteration 2 — Instructor lived-experience: what actually breaks in “class chat” workflows

### Pain points / objections
**Class Chat AI (adjacent: Ed Discussion vs Slack/Discord vs Piazza)**
- Pain points with Slack in class: too many DMs; lack of math typesetting; anonymity friction; hard to endorse answers.
- Pain points with Ed: no free tier; no standalone app; can’t DM; “one more platform” cognitive load.
- Pricing objection surfaced: **$6 per student per course**.
- Source: https://stattlc.com/2022/07/29/using-ed-discussion-as-a-course-communication-tool/

### Workflow expectations
- “Must-have primitives” for class Q&A:
  - categories/subcategories, anonymous posting (with clarity on who can see identity), LaTeX, endorsed answers, megathreads with resolved state, LTI integration.
- Source: https://stattlc.com/2022/07/29/using-ed-discussion-as-a-course-communication-tool/

### Channel / marketing tactics
- Adoption motion is instructor-led, but procurement is department/institution-led when cost exists.
- Implication: Class Chat AI should ship “rollout collateral” (1-page privacy, syllabus blurb, LMS integration guide) to unblock adoption.
- Source (procurement norm evidence): https://stattlc.com/2022/07/29/using-ed-discussion-as-a-course-communication-tool/

### Trust / privacy concerns
- Piazza’s privacy controversy is an adoption lever (switching pressure).
- Source: https://stanforddaily.com/2020/10/04/concerned-with-piazzas-data-privacy-management-some-professors-look-to-alternative-discussion-forums/
