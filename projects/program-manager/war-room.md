## Current GTM hypotheses (top 6)

### AIStudyBuddy
1) **Trust wedge:** “Citations + jump-to-page” increases signup intent vs generic “chat with PDFs”.
2) **Exam-week urgency:** time-boxed “midterm survival kit” messaging outperforms always-on studying messaging.
3) **Channel:** Reddit validation posts + comments (with a short demo) can produce first 25–50 waitlist signups.

### Agent for X
4) **Voice wedge:** showing 3 “you wrote this → agent rewrote in your voice” examples drives higher conversion than feature lists.
5) **Offer:** “Send me a transcript; I’ll draft 3 posts free” beats “join the waitlist” for early adopters.
6) **Channel:** build-in-public before/after posts on X daily for 14 days produces ≥10 qualified inbound DMs.
