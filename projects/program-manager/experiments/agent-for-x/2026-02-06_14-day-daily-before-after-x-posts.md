# Experiment — Agent for X: 14-Day Daily Before/After X Posts

**Date:** 2026-02-06

## Hypothesis
If we post one high-quality before/after example daily for 14 days, then we will generate ≥10 qualified inbound DMs because repetition + proof builds trust and awareness.

## Funnel stage
Acquisition

## Metric
- **Primary:** # inbound DMs from qualified creators
- **Secondary:** profile visits/day, link clicks, waitlist submits

## Segment
Creators and founder-creators on X.

## Test design
- **Type:** content cadence test
- **Asset format:**
  - 1 hook line
  - 1 “raw idea” screenshot/text
  - 1 “draft in your voice” output
  - 1 CTA (“Want me to do yours?”)
- **Cadence:** 1 post/day for 14 consecutive days
- **Duration:** 14 days

## Stop rule
Pause if:
- we cannot maintain example quality, or
- after 7 posts there are 0 qualified replies/DMs (revise hook + proof format).

## Result
Pending

## What we do next
- If win: turn into an always-on weekly schedule + repurpose into landing page examples.
- If loss: change proof format (video/gif), or switch to threads with more context.
