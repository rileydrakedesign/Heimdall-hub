# Experiment — Agent for X: "Send transcript → get 3 drafts" Outreach

**Date:** 2026-02-06

## Hypothesis
If we offer a concrete service-style CTA (“send me a transcript; I’ll draft 3 posts in your voice”), then we will start more high-intent conversations than a waitlist CTA because the user gets immediate value and can judge voice quality.

## Funnel stage
Acquisition → Activation proxy (conversation → first output)

## Metric
- **Primary:** # qualified creator conversations started (DM threads)
- **Secondary:** # who send a transcript/notes; # who ask to try the product; waitlist submits

## Segment
Creators posting on X (10k–250k followers) and founder-creators.

## Test design
- **Type:** outbound + reply-driven
- **Volume target:** 30 outreach attempts over 7 days
- **Script:**
  - 1 sentence: what we’re building
  - 1 sentence: offer (3 drafts)
  - 1 ask: “Want to try it with your last transcript/notes?”
- **Sample size proxy:** 30 attempts OR 7-day time-box
- **Duration:** 7 days

## Stop rule
Stop if:
- <3 qualified conversations after 15 attempts (revise targeting and script), or
- replies indicate strong negative sentiment around “automation” framing.

## Result
Pending

## What we do next
- If win: productize the workflow into a self-serve “upload → drafts” path.
- If loss: adjust ICP (smaller creators) or switch offer to “1 thread rewrite” (lower effort).
