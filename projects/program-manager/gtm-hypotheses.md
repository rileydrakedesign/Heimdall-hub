# GTM Hypotheses (Portfolio)

**Purpose:** a living bank of measurable GTM bets. Keep it small, crisp, and testable.

**Rules**
- A hypothesis must include: **segment**, **claim**, **channel**, **asset**, **metric**, **time-box**.
- If we can’t measure it in 7–14 days, rewrite it.

---

## AIStudyBuddy — hypotheses

### H1 — Trust wedge beats generic PDF chat
- **Segment:** students who already tried ChatGPT for homework and got burned
- **Claim:** “answers with citations + jump-to-page” increases signup intent vs generic “chat with PDFs”.
- **Channel:** landing page + demo clip
- **Asset:** 15s screen recording showing citation + click-to-page
- **Primary metric:** waitlist conversion rate (visit→submit)
- **Success threshold:** +30% relative lift vs control headline (or ≥8% CVR baseline)
- **Time-box:** 7 days

### H2 — Exam-week urgency improves activation
- **Segment:** students within 10 days of an exam
- **Claim:** exam-oriented onboarding (“upload study guide → practice questions with citations”) increases time-to-first-value.
- **Channel:** onboarding sequence
- **Asset:** 3-step checklist inside app
- **Primary metric:** % who get first cited answer within 5 minutes
- **Success threshold:** ≥50% of new users
- **Time-box:** 14 days

### H3 — Reddit comments outperform top-level posts
- **Segment:** r/college / r/studytips users asking for study help
- **Claim:** helpful comment + demo link yields more qualified signups than standalone promotion posts.
- **Channel:** Reddit comments
- **Asset:** “helpful comment template” + single demo link
- **Primary metric:** qualified waitlist signups (self-reported “have class PDFs”)
- **Success threshold:** 10+ qualified signups/week
- **Time-box:** 14 days

## Agent for X — hypotheses

### H4 — Before/after proof drives conversion
- **Segment:** creators who value voice + consistency (10k–250k followers)
- **Claim:** showing 3 before/after examples (raw → in-your-voice) increases conversion vs feature carousel.
- **Channel:** landing page + X thread
- **Asset:** before/after pack (see template)
- **Primary metric:** waitlist conversion rate
- **Success threshold:** ≥10% CVR on targeted traffic OR +30% relative lift
- **Time-box:** 7 days

### H5 — Service offer produces faster early adopters
- **Segment:** founder-creators posting inconsistently
- **Claim:** offering “send a transcript, get 3 drafts” yields more high-intent conversations than waitlist-only CTA.
- **Channel:** X DMs + replies
- **Asset:** outreach script + before/after sample
- **Primary metric:** # of qualified DM threads (meet ICP + agrees to test)
- **Success threshold:** 10 qualified DM threads / 14 days
- **Time-box:** 14 days

### H6 — Daily build-in-public loop creates inbound
- **Segment:** builders/creators following maker Twitter
- **Claim:** 14 consecutive days of before/after posts creates ≥10 inbound DMs from creators.
- **Channel:** X
- **Asset:** daily post template + 1 screenshot/gif each
- **Primary metric:** inbound DMs (qualified)
- **Success threshold:** 10 qualified DMs / 14 days
- **Time-box:** 14 days
