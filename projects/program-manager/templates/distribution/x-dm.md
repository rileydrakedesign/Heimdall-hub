# Template — X DM Outreach

**Goal:** start a conversation with a concrete offer, not a pitch.

Hi <name> — I’m building a tool that turns raw notes/transcripts into X posts *in your voice*.

If you want, send me your last transcript (or even messy bullets) and I’ll draft 3 posts you can publish.

No worries if not — I’m collecting feedback from creators who care about voice + consistency.
