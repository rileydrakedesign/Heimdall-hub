Anthropic released a new plugin for Claude Cowork that can track compliance and review legal documents.

The update didn't make much noise outside the legal industry when it launched Friday, but it triggered a sell-off in legal software and publishing stocks.

Wolters Kluwer dropped 13%, RELX fell 15%, LegalZoom dropped 18%, and Thomson Reuters fell 19%.

All four stocks are now down at least 20% year-to-date.

RELX owns LexisNexis.
Thomson Reuters owns Westlaw.

My Take

Investors are repricing what it means for companies whose business model depends on selling access to legal research and document services.

These are not speculative startups.

Thomson Reuters and RELX are massive, established companies.

When an AI tool can do in minutes what used to require expensive subscriptions and billable associate hours, the math changes.

The stocks had already been drifting lower all year.

The Anthropic announcement accelerated what was already underway.

Venture capital has been pouring money into legal tech startups betting on this disruption.

The incumbents are starting to look vulnerable and the market is adjusting accordingly.
