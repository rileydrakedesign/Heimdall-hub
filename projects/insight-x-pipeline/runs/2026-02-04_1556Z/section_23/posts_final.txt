Post 2

brain-computer interfaces will change our tech interactions.

but skepticism remains about their effectiveness.

- human cognition struggles with non-visual information
- processing data without visual cues is challenging
- intuitive interfaces are essential for success

if you develop these technologies, consider user experience.
understand the limits of human perception.
