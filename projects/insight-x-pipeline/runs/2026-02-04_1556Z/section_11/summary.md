## Section Summary: Tool Use

- Tool use in LLMs can mitigate but not completely eliminate hallucinations.
- Breaking down tasks recursively can enhance LLM performance, especially in complex scenarios.
- The future of open models relies on innovative tool use and orchestration.
- Trust remains a critical barrier for LLMs handling sensitive tasks.
- Open and closed models differ significantly in their tool integration, affecting usability.
- Open-source tools can empower LLMs to delegate tasks more effectively, enhancing problem-solving capabilities.

### Key Insights

1. **Tool use may reduce hallucination in LLMs, but it won't eliminate it entirely.**
   - **Why it matters:** Developers must be aware of this limitation to ensure accuracy in applications.
   - **Quote:** "Not solve it, but reduce it... it still needs to find the right website and get the right information." *(02:35:01)*

2. **Recursive task breakdown could enhance LLM performance by allowing them to tackle complex problems in manageable pieces.**
   - **Why it matters:** This method can lead to more effective applications in research and academia.
   - **Quote:** "Instead of having the LLM solve all of it in, like, one shot... you break it down into sub-tasks." *(02:36:16)*

3. **The future of open models may hinge on their ability to innovate in tool use and orchestration.**
   - **Why it matters:** This could drive breakthroughs that enhance the functionality of open-source LLMs.
   - **Quote:** "It’s gonna necessitate, like, a bit more flexible and potentially interesting model that might work with this recursive idea..." *(02:38:01)*

4. **Trust is a significant barrier to allowing LLMs access to sensitive tasks like email management.**
   - **Why it matters:** Founders must prioritize user trust when designing LLM applications.
   - **Quote:** "I don't know if I would today give an LLM access to my emails, right? I mean, this is, like, a huge risk." *(02:36:57)*

5. **Open and closed models utilize tools in fundamentally different ways, impacting their usability and flexibility.**
   - **Why it matters:** This distinction can guide developers in selecting the right model for their needs.
   - **Quote:** "Open models... need to be useful for multiple tools, for multiple use cases, which is really hard..." *(02:37:36)*

6. **Open-source tools are poised to unlock new capabilities in LLMs by allowing them to delegate tasks more effectively.**
   - **Why it matters:** This could shift LLMs from mere memorization to actual problem-solving.
   - **Quote:** "I think we will see more of that in the open-source tooling. And I think, I mean, it is a huge unlock..." *(02:34:37)*

7. **Closed models can offer a more seamless user experience by deeply integrating specific tools.**
   - **Why it matters:** This could enhance performance in tasks involving mixed public and private information.
   - **Quote:** "You're deeply integrating the specific tool into your experience..." *(02:37:36)*

8. **The initial rush to develop tool use in LLMs has left open models at a disadvantage compared to their closed counterparts.**
   - **Why it matters:** Recognizing this gap can inform strategic decisions for developers in the open-source community.
   - **Quote:** "I think initially cause there was such a rush to get these tool use working that the open models were on the back foot..." *(02:38:01)*
