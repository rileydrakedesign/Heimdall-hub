## Section Summary: How AI is Trained: Pre-training, Mid-training, and Post-training

- AI training involves a complex interplay of data quality, ethical considerations, and legal constraints.
- High-quality synthetic data can significantly improve training efficiency, emphasizing the importance of data curation over sheer quantity.
- The ethical implications of AI training are profound, particularly concerning vulnerable populations and the potential for misuse.
- Human verification remains essential in the data generation process to maintain quality and relevance.
- Over-reliance on AI tools can hinder personal development and expertise among builders and developers.
