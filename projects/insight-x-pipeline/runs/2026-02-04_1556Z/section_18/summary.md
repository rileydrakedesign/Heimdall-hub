## Summary: How AI Will Make Money

- AI has the potential to create unique content in underrepresented niches, opening new market opportunities.
- Initial advertising efforts in AI may struggle, causing companies to tread cautiously and delaying effective monetization.
- Enhanced targeting capabilities of AI-driven advertising could empower small businesses, leveling the playing field against larger competitors.
- Long-term ad revenue can sustain R&D, fostering a cycle of innovation and improved AI models.
- The current AI landscape is heavily reliant on ad revenue, influencing the monetization strategies of new entrants.
- Fear of reputational damage and poor ad performance is hindering companies from fully embracing AI advertising.
