Post 1

safety in robotics is often overlooked.

this is a major concern for real-world applications.

1/ failures in robotic systems can lead to severe consequences
2/ the stakes are high when robots operate in homes and public spaces
3/ ensuring safety must be a priority in design and deployment

you cannot afford to fail in this area. focus on rigorous testing and validation.

2/ if these expectations are not met, investment may dwindle
3/ sustained interest relies on delivering tangible results

be cautious with projections. focus on achievable milestones to maintain credibility.
