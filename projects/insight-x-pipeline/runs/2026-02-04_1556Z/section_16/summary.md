## Summary: Will AI Replace Programmers?

- AI may automate certain coding tasks, but human oversight remains essential for complex systems.
- The complexity of existing codebases can limit AI's ability to autonomously add features, emphasizing the need for clean code.
- Effective AI integration in programming requires a shift in human skills and understanding of AI capabilities.
- The future of software development may focus more on intent rather than syntax, democratizing the field for non-programmers.
- Smaller companies could have an edge in adopting AI due to less complex systems, altering competitive dynamics in tech.
