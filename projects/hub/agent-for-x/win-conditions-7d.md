# 7-Day Win Conditions (Proposal) — Agent for X

Date: 2026-02-06

## North Star (7 days)
Prove a repeatable **"1 input → 5 outputs"** loop that a real person will use again.

### Win condition A — Delivery proof
- Deliver **3 complete client-style packs** (from real or demo inputs):
  - 1× thread (7–10 tweets)
  - 3× single posts
  - 1× CTA post
  - with citations/quotes + “why it matters” framing
- Each pack takes **≤ 90 minutes** end-to-end (including review) by Day 7.

### Win condition B — Distribution proof
- Publish **10 posts** from the system (manual posting is fine) across X in 7 days.
- Hit **≥ 20 total meaningful engagements** (sum of replies + DMs + link clicks + email captures) OR **≥ 2 qualified conversations**.
  - “Qualified” = the person matches ICP and asks about pricing/process/timing.

### Win condition C — Product proof
- A prospect can:
  1) understand the offer in **≤ 20 seconds** (landing),
  2) submit an intake in **≤ 2 minutes**,
  3) receive a first draft within **24 hours**.

### Win condition D — Internal proof (ops)
- KPI tracking + experiment register updated daily (even if results are zero).
- One weekly reset completes in **≤ 30 minutes**.

## Constraints / Assumptions
- Posting can be manual; automation is optional for this proof.
- Quality > volume: do not ship content without a defensible quote/source.

## REQUIRED_INPUT
- ICP choice + 2–3 example creators/accounts to emulate.
- A real "before" artifact (transcript/notes) and the desired "after" style.
