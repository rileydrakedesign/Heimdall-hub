# Beta Onboarding Checklist — Agent for X

## Pre-qualify (2 minutes)
- [ ] Do they already publish on X? (link)
- [ ] Do they have raw material? (calls, Looms, podcasts)
- [ ] Do they want 1 pack/week minimum?

## Onboarding steps
1) [ ] Collect intake (see `intake-workflow.md`).
2) [ ] Collect 2–3 voice examples.
3) [ ] Confirm topic lane + 1 audience.
4) [ ] Confirm constraints (things to avoid, claims).
5) [ ] Confirm turnaround time + delivery format.

## First delivery
- [ ] Deliver pack #1 with:
  - [ ] Evidence/quotes section
  - [ ] 1 thread
  - [ ] 3 singles
  - [ ] 1 CTA
- [ ] Ask 3Q feedback (`feedback-form-3q.md`).

## After feedback
- [ ] Update their style notes (bullets).
- [ ] Record “wins”: which post they would publish.
- [ ] Offer next pack schedule.

## REQUIRED_INPUT
- Pricing/positioning for beta (free, discounted, or paid).
